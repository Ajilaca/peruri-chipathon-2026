# Phase 9I item 4 report: polynomial loads behind the K-PKE engine (configuration K4 = `mlkem_core4`)

- Status: **DONE** (STOP after the step). Branch `phase9m-optimisation`, not committed at the time of writing. New files only (the Phase 6-8 files and `mlkem_core3` are not edited): `rtl/mlkem/mlkem_core4.sv`, `rtl/mlkem/mlkem_ldpoly2o.sv`, `rtl/mlkem/mlkem_ctl_rom3.sv` (generated by `scripts/build/gen_mlkem_ctl_rom3.py` from `tb/golden/mlkem_ctl_model3.py`), `rtl/sched/kpke_sched_smp4.sv`, `rtl/sched/kpke_smp_top_s10o.sv`. Plan and rule: `test_plan_9i4.md` (**process disclosure:** the RTL and the first core simulation were written before the plan; the rule of section 5 was written before any Quartus compile and before the control runs, and is not tuned to the result). Kernel-only static timing with virtual pins; not a system on the board. Twelve Quartus compiles, all `rc=0`, one at a time.

## 1. Result
**The rule of the plan adopts K4.** Loads of polynomials now run while the K-PKE engine computes; the controller starts the engine program, loads the other polynomials behind it (a per-slot interlock holds back an engine operation that needs a slot not yet loaded) and joins. Cycles (profile inputs, simulation): KeyGen 8,416 (unchanged), **Encaps 9,611 (-639, -6.2 %)**, **Decaps 12,989 (-2,630, -16.8 %)** against K3 (10,250 and 15,619). Median Fmax at 15 ns is 76.665 MHz against 77.555 MHz of K3 (-0.89 MHz, inside the seed spread). Latency at 15 ns (cycles / median Fmax, perhitungan tim): **109.8 / 125.4 / 169.4 us** against 108.5 / 132.2 / 201.4 us of K3 (+1.2 %, -5.1 %, -15.9 %). ALM median +106.5 (40 ns) and +187.0 (15 ns); registers, RAM blocks and DSP: see section 2. ACVP is 100 % on both simulators. **Limit of the evidence: the bounded formal proof P1 and the negative control NC-E1-4 timed out; they have no formal result (section 3).**

## 4. Rule of the plan (section 5), applied to files
1. V1-V7 pass: met (the two wrong controls were corrected and re-run, stated in the sim file). V8 is not part of the rule; its limit is stated above.
2. Timing at 40 ns and at 15 ns at every seed 1-6: met (6 of 6 and 6 of 6).
3. ALM median at most 20,000: met (14,222.0 and 14,480.0).
4. Encaps and Decaps latency lower than K3; KeyGen not more than +3 %: met (Encaps -5.15 %, Decaps -15.87 %, KeyGen +1.16 %).
5. Seeds met at 15 ns: 6 of 6. Median Fmax 76.665 against 77.555 MHz (-0.890); spread K4 11.56 MHz, K3 6.35 MHz; **the drop is not larger than the spread, so it is not called a drop**; the lowest seed of K4 (69.43 MHz) is 3.6 MHz below the lowest of K3 (73.02 MHz).
The rule adopts. The team decides (Proposed ADR 0043). The base of the measurement is K3 (ADR 0042, Proposed, not adopted by its rule as written).

## 5. Estimates against measurements (written in the plan before the measurements)
- Cycles Encaps about -660 and Decaps about -2,630, KeyGen unchanged: **met** (-639, -2,630, 0).
- ALM +50 to +300: **met** (+106.5 at 40 ns, +187.0 at 15 ns).

- Latency at the K3 Fmax 108.5 / about 123.6 / about 167.5 us: at the K4 median Fmax the measured values are 109.8 / 125.4 / 169.4 us (the estimate assumed the K3 Fmax; the difference is the 0.89 MHz).

## 6. Findings
1. **The overlap works as designed and is verified**: bit-exact against the golden model on 60 ACVP vectors and 20 random cases on two simulators; every wait depends on fixed-length blocks (loader, engine) or on the public rejection sampling of A, never on a secret (cycle counts of Encaps and Decaps identical across the tested inputs).
2. **The saving is in Encaps and Decaps** (loads behind the engine); KeyGen has no loads to hide and is unchanged. Decaps gains more because it loads more polynomials (decrypt then re-encrypt).
3. **The low Fmax seed of K4 is not an item-4 path.** The worst path of the smallest-slack seed (69.43 MHz) belongs to the registered issue address of S2b (`start_go` depends on the sequencer's counter through the host write enable). Item 4 has not made a new critical path in the 300 worst paths of that seed.
4. **Formal evidence is incomplete for P1 and NC-E1-4.** P1 (the engine is not busy in STP and SDL) rests on simulation (ACVP, constant cycles, controls `ncjoin` and `ncgrant`) and on the negative control NC-JOIN4 that fails in the formal run.
5. **Cost:** about 190 ALM (+1.3 %) and a few dozen registers; no RAM block or DSP.

## 7. What this does not show
No board result; no speed claim against software; kernel-only static timing with virtual pins; seed noise of 3 to 11 MHz limits any Fmax statement below that size; constant time means cycle-count invariance only; ACVP and the controls are simulation; the formal model uses protocol stubs, and P1 and NC-E1-4 have no formal result; stores are still in series with the engine and KeyGen is unchanged (possible later steps); one seed (K4-15-s6) was analysed for the paths.
