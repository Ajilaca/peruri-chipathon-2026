# Phase 4 fitter-seed sweep, P = 4 and P = 6 (input to ADR 0008)

- Date (UTC): 2026-10-01. Requested by the team after ADR 0008 was proposed, because the ALM margins (P = 4: 39
  below the 10,478 budget, P = 6: 27 above it) were smaller than the fitter-packing swing seen in Phase 3.
- What changed: only `set_global_assignment -name SEED <n>` (n = 2..6) in copies of `C3-P4.qsf` / `C3-P6.qsf`
  (`quartus/phase04_pipeline_c3/C3-P{4,6}-s{2..6}.qsf`). Seed 1 is the default used by the measured revisions
  (confirmed in their fitter reports). RTL (git 7947c0c), SDC (40.000 ns) and every other assignment identical.
- Tool: Quartus Prime Lite 25.1std.0 Build 1129. Per-seed evidence extracted with `extract_quartus_report.py` into
  `evidence/phase04/seed_sweep/`; table below generated by `python3 scripts/quartus/phase4_seed_sweep_summary.py`.
- This sweep does **not** change the measured worksheet (`selection_worksheet.md`), ADR 0007's rule or
  the test plan, which fixes the default seed. It is information for the team's decision on ADR 0008.

## Reading the results
- **Timing is not the issue.** All 12 compiles meet 40.000 ns (setup and hold, every corner). P = 4 lowest-slow-corner
  Fmax ranges 30.60–33.00 MHz across seeds, P = 6 32.60–34.20 MHz.
- **The ALM condition is.** P = 6 is over the 10,478 budget at **every** seed (10,484–10,516), so P = 6 is never a
  candidate. P = 4 ranges 10,439–10,503 ALM and is within budget at 4 of 6 seeds; at seeds 2 and 3 it is over
  (by 25 and by 1 ALM), and at those seeds there is no candidate at all.
- The per-seed rule never selects anything other than P = 4; it either selects P = 4 or selects nothing.
- Registers vary slightly with the seed (register duplication / packing by the fitter); M10K (26 for P = 4, 29 for
  P = 6) and DSP (9) do not.
- Seed-to-seed spread here is 64 ALM (P = 4) and 32 ALM (P = 6). That is much smaller than the ~370 ALM swing
  recorded in Phase 3 (K2 at L = 1, `docs/results/phase03.md`); this sweep does not explain that swing and
  does not show that later RTL changes will stay within 64 ALM.

## Process notes (stated plainly)
- The first sweep attempt was cut off at seed 4 when the session ended; the half-written seed-4 databases were
  corrupted. Two chains were then run in parallel, which made Quartus rewrite the shared `.qpf` concurrently and
  drop a revision line (P4 seed 5 failed with "Revision does not exist"). The three affected compiles (P4 s4,
  P6 s4, P4 s5) were rerun one at a time after deleting their databases and restoring the `.qpf`; their fitter
  reports confirm seed 4 / 4 / 5. The seven compiles that succeeded in the parallel run were checked for the right
  seed and top entity in their own reports (all correct). No result was taken from a failed run.
- Critical warnings in all 10 new compiles: only 15725 (virtual-pin clock), as in every revision of Phases 1–4;
  no 332148 (timing met).
